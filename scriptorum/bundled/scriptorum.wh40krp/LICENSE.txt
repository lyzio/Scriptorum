It uses no text, artwork or logos from any publisher. Its frames and
ornaments are the project's own drawings (tools/make_more_themes.py).

Fonts in fonts/ keep their own licences, each in the licence file beside the font:
Spectral and Share Tech Mono (SIL OFL 1.1) from Google Fonts; Grenze Gotisch (SIL OFL 1.1), static weights from its own project (github.com/Omnibus-Type/Grenze-Gotisch).

The relationship chart uses fletcher 0.5.8 (MIT), CeTZ 0.3.4
(LGPL-3.0-or-later) and oxifmt 0.2.1 (MIT-0) in vendor/, each with its licence file.
