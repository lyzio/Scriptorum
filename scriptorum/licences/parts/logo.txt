The Scriptorum logo

The logo (a twenty-sided die over a crossed pen and scroll, with the word
Scriptorum on a ribbon) was made with an AI image tool for the Scriptorum
project, and chosen by the project manager on 18 November 2026. It is shown
in the About window, on the welcome screen, in the start-up window and in the
README. The files are scriptorum/ui/logo.png (512 pixels, used by the app)
and docs/logo/scriptorum-logo.png (the original, 1254 pixels).
